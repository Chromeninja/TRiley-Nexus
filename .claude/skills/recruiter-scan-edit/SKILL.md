## Purpose

Make portfolio content faster to scan without flattening the nuance.

## Editing priorities

1. Put the strongest proof first.
2. Prefer concrete scope over abstract adjectives.
3. Make role fit obvious.
4. Keep sentences shorter.
5. Use bullets for proof points.
6. Remove repeated phrasing.
7. Replace internal jargon with portable language.

## Preferred phrases

Use where accurate:
- technical program leadership
- product operations
- operational systems
- roadmap ownership
- requirements quality
- release planning
- stakeholder alignment
- workflow automation
- governance systems
- staff-adopted tooling
- production-used workflows
- field-tested operating model
- privacy-conscious analytics
- cross-functional delivery

## Avoid overusing

- messy work
- people can actually use
- practical
- operating layer
- clearer
- systems
- helped with
- passionate

## Output expectations

When editing, produce direct replacement text.
When reviewing, identify:
- what to keep
- what to cut
- what to move higher
- what to quantify
